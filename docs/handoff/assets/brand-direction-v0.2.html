Mark · a K whose arm ends at the point of human decision

No rounded tile, no gradient. A plain, heavy letterform that works in one color at 16 px, with one accent dot. The dot is the brand idea: the point where the agent stops and a person decides.

Color · one chromatic color, used with discipline
Ink#0A0D12Most of every screen
Paper#F5F6F7
Keel#2DD4BF#0F766E on light
Signal#F5B547#B45309 on light
Stop#F87171#DC2626 on light

Keel teal marks the brand and agent activity. Signal amber appears only when a human must decide. Stop red is only for denied or failed. Every state also carries a shape and a word, never color alone. Contrast measured at 4.5:1 or better for text in both themes.

Type · Archivo for voice, Instrument Sans for interface, Martian Mono for evidence
Every action, on the record.
Archivo 800, condensed width

Headlines and the wordmark. Confident and compact, like transit signage, not a template.

Instrument Sans

Interface and body text. Quiet and legible at 12 to 16 px in long sessions.

Martian Mono
